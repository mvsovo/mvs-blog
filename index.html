Weijun Li

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Informatics at 🐜 UC Irvine, advised by Daniel Epstein in the 🥧 PIE Lab.

My research sits at the intersection of human-centered AI, health, and social computing. I build and study interactive systems that help people make sense of information about themselves, their health, and their everyday lives. I examine how these systems should interpret, present, and communicate personal information to support understanding, reflection, and decision making. I combine qualitative research, interactive system design, and real-world deployment.

My work appears at CHI, CSCW, UIST, and AMIA. Before Irvine I did my master's at Zhejiang University with Prof. Lingyun Sun and Prof. Shi Chen. Earlier, I competed in algorithm contests and worked as a full-stack developer during my undergraduate studies.

Outside research I am usually 🚴‍♀️🌸 out on my bike, or at home with my favourite cat Bubbles and my favourite people. Always happy to chat about research or bikes.
